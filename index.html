Maths Quest fits around your family, with personal practice and a clear view of their progress.

Rooted in the Irish curriculum ＋

Lessons and practice draw on Ireland’s primary maths curriculum for 1st–6th Class. Choose the topics being covered at school, then explore them through play, visual explanations and helpful examples.

Their own pace. Their own progress. ＋

Personal topic plans revisit tricky ideas and keep familiar skills fresh. The protected grown-ups area shows topic progress and completed quests, with controls for sound, celebrations, quest length and optional read-aloud.

Turbo Tables offers a timed challenge against computer opponents. Supported practice gives children more space to think. There are no public leaderboards.

No account needed. Save if you want to. ＋

Guest profiles and progress stay on this device. Optional Google sign-in lets a parent save learner profiles, photos, preferences and completed results privately across devices. Changes save when online.

There is no advertising or upgrade prompting aimed at children. Cookie-free analytics count anonymous activity; learner names, photos, answers, scores and progress are not sent to analytics.
